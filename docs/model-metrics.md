# Category classifier metrics

Generated by `ai/classify.py`. Maps NTSB probable-cause text to ICAO CICTT
occurrence categories so US and Canadian occurrences share one taxonomy.

- **Label source:** gpt-4.1-mini drafts, NOT reviewed by a person (data/labelling_sheet_drafted.xlsx)
- **Labelled rows:** 1500 (NTSB corpus, July 2022 - Dec 2024, stratified by findings)
- **Model:** TF-IDF (1-2 grams) + logistic regression, class_weight=balanced
- **Split:** stratified 80/20 (1192 train / 298 test), seed 42
- **Macro F1:** 0.485
- **Weighted F1:** 0.718
- **Accuracy:** 0.701 (always guessing the most common category: 0.201)

Because the labels come from an LLM and were not reviewed, these scores measure how
closely the small model reproduces gpt-4.1-mini's labels, not accuracy against ground
truth. Macro F1 on a 60-row test set is also noisy (a few rows swing it by several points).

Categories with fewer than 5 labels were left out as too rare to learn: MED (2), GTOW (1), AMAN (1), EXTL (1), ICE (1), CABIN (1), SEC (1), USOS (1), EVAC (1).
Occurrences of those kinds are assigned the nearest learned category, so rare categories
are under-counted for NTSB.

Predictions written for 14,574 NTSB occurrences with text, stored with
category_source = 'model' and the predicted probability in category_confidence.
